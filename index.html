04Aksar sawal

Sawal? Jawab hazir

Kul kharcha kitna hoga? Koi hidden charges?

Nahi. Jo rate package me likha hai, wahi final hai. Hosting wala kharcha pehle din se alag aur saaf likha hota hai — baad me koi surprise bill nahi.

Payment kaise karun?

JazzCash, EasyPaisa ya bank transfer. Kaam 50% advance se shuru hota hai, baqi 50% sirf tab jab website live ho jaye.

Website kitne din me bane gi?

Aam tor par 1–2 haftay, package aur products ki tadaad par depend karta hai. Pehli chat me hi main pakka waqt bata dunga — aur wahi waqt milega.

Mujhe kya dena hoga?

Bas apne products ki photos aur rates. Phone se li hui photos se bhi shuruaat ho sakti hai — main step by step guide kar dunga.

Website meri apni hogi?

100% aap ki. Domain aur website aap ke naam par — koi lock-in nahi. Kal ko aap kisi aur se bhi kaam karwa sakte hain.

Urdu me website ho sakti hai?

Bilkul. Urdu, English ya dono ka mix — jaise aap ke customers ke liye asan ho.

Main to Instagram par hi bechta hun — website kyun?

Instagram par order DMs me gum hota hai aur bharosa mushkil banta hai. Website se Google par log aap ko dhoond sakte hain, bara order dene wala customer bharosa karta hai, aur har order ka record aap ke paas rehta hai.
