Website banwana hai? Pehle ye parh lein

Ye guide har us business owner ke liye hai jo pehli dafa website banwa raha hai — asal cost, asal waqt, aur wo baatein jo aksar log pehle nahi batate.

1 — Cost kis cheez par depend karti hai?

Website ki qeemat 4 cheezon se banti hai: design kitna custom hai, kitne pages/products hain, kaun se features chahiye (order system, tracking, admin panel), aur hosting/domain ka saal bhar ka kharcha. Isi liye ek hi "website" kisi ke liye Rs. 10,000 ki hai aur kisi ke liye Rs. 50,000 ki.

2 — Pakistan me aam rates kya hain?

Chhota freelancer kabhi kabhi Rs. 5,000–15,000 me basic site bana deta hai (aksar baghair support ke). Bari agencies yahi kaam Rs. 35,000 se shuru karti hain. Koclify par packages Rs. 9,999 se shuru hain — kyunke aap seedha founder se kaam karte hain, office aur sales team ka kharcha rate me nahi jata.

3 — Kitna waqt lagta hai?

Aam tor par 1–2 haftay. Sab se zyada waqt products ki photos aur rates jama karne me lagta hai — ye aap ke paas pehle se tayyar hon to kaam aur tez ho jata hai.

4 — Aap ko kya tayyar rakhna hai?

Bas teen cheezein: products ki photos (phone ki bhi chalengi), un ke rates, aur business ka naam/logo agar hai. Baqi sab — likhai, design, setup — banane wale ka kaam hai.

5 — Dhoke ki 3 nishaniyan

Rate likh kar na dena

Jo pehle din saaf likha hua rate na de, baad me "ye extra, wo extra" nikalta hai.

Poora advance pehle

Kaam shuru hue baghair 100% advance mangna khatre ki ghanti hai. Saheeh tareeqa: aadha shuru me, aadha live hone par.

Website apne naam par rakhna

Domain aur website hamesha AAP ke naam par honi chahiye — warna kal ko aap bandhe hue hain.

6 — Steps kya hote hain?

WhatsApp par baat → likha hua saaf rate → 50% advance → apne phone par live preview → pasand aye to live → baqi 50% aur chalane ki training. Bas. Is se zyada mushkil koi bhi step nahi hona chahiye.
